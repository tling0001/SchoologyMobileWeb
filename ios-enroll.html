Register your iPhone

Prepare your iPhone for installing the QR-distributed Schoology app.

This registers the device with Apple for Ad Hoc app distribution. It does not enroll the phone in mobile-device management or install a management profile.

Open this page on your computer, then scan the QR code with your iPhone camera. On the iPhone, follow the prompts to download and install the temporary enrollment profile.

Enrollment links expire after 30 minutes and can be used once.

Waiting for iPhone…

Keep this page open while you install the temporary profile on your iPhone.

  1. Use Safari on the iPhone to open the enrollment link.
  2. Tap Allow to download the temporary profile, then open Settings to install it.
  3. Keep this page open until registration and the iPhone build have been queued.

For security, this page never asks you to type your UDID. The server must validate Apple's signed device response before registering the device.
